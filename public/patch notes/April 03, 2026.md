New Flow: Inventory & Daily Remittance Interaction
Below is the updated end-to-end workflow that governs how Inventory and Daily Remittance interact per shift.
Step-by-Step Flow

1. Add New Inventory — Staff creates a new inventory for the current shift. If this is a continuation from a previous shift, the ending quantities carry over as beginning quantities (everything else resets to zero).
2. Sales Period — The inventory is now open. Staff processes orders normally. All sales, usage, and transactions are tracked against this open inventory.
3. Close Inventory — Staff closes the inventory to prevent any further tampering or edits to the data. The inventory becomes read-only.
4. Send Inventory Report — Staff clicks "Send Report". The system calculates all data from the time the inventory was opened to when it was closed. Once the report is sent, the inventory is permanently locked and cannot be reopened.
5. Redirect to Daily Remittance — After the report is processed, the staff is automatically redirected to the Daily Remittance page. The remittance only calculates orders within the open-to-close time window of that inventory and verifies ownership of each order against the current staff account.
6. Send Remittance — Staff reviews and sends the remittance. Once processed, the staff can return to the Inventory page.
7. New Shift → New Inventory — Staff creates a new inventory for the next shift. Ending quantities from the previous inventory carry over as the beginning for the new one. The cycle repeats.
